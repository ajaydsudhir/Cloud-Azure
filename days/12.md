## Solution
1. Log in to the Azure portal using the provided credentials.
2. Navigate to the "Virtual Machines" section in the Azure portal.
3. Locate the virtual machine named `devops-vm` in the list of virtual machines.
4. Click on the `devops-vm` to open its details page.
5. In the left-hand menu, click on "Tags" under the "Settings" section.
6. In the "Name" field, enter `Environment`.
7. In the "Value" field, enter `dev`.
8. Click on the "Save" button to save the tag.

## Notes
- `Tags` are key-value pairs that help organize and manage Azure resources which are used for cost management, automation, and resource organization.
