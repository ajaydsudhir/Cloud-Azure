## Solution
1. Log in to the Azure portal using the provided credentials.
2. Navigate to the "Virtual Machines" section and select the VM named `xfusion-vm`.
3. In the VM's menu, click on "Disks" under the "Settings" section.
4. Click on the "Attach existing disk" button.
5. In the "Attach existing disk" pane, select the managed disk named `xfusion-disk` from the list of available disks.
6. Click "Apply" to attach the disk to the VM.
7. Wait for the operation to complete and ensure that the disk is now listed under the "Disks" section of the VM.
